- Tourists struggle to find authentic cultural experiences
- Local artisans lack direct market access
- Heritage sites lack engaging, multi-language content
- No verification system for authentic crafts
- Language barriers limit cultural immersion

## The Solution:
**YatriAI - An AI-powered platform that:**
- Provides personalized, context-aware travel guidance
- Connects tourists directly with verified local artisans via blockchain
- Offers immersive audio guides in multiple languages
- Preserves heritage through NFTs and transparent donations
- Supports local communities through direct marketplace access

## Why Now:
- Post-pandemic surge in domestic tourism
- Growing interest in heritage and cultural experiences
- Blockchain adoption for authenticity verification
- AI technology mature enough for personalized experiences
- Mobile-first generation ready for immersive travel apps
