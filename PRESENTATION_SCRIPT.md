## 📋 What is YatriAI? (1 minute)

**"YatriAI is a comprehensive tourism platform that combines artificial intelligence, blockchain technology, and immersive experiences to create personalized journeys through India's cultural heritage. The name 'Yatri' means 'traveler' in Sanskrit, and we're empowering every traveler to become a cultural explorer."**

**Key Points:**
- Heritage-focused tourism platform
- AI-powered personalization
- Multi-language support (English, Hindi, Bengali, Santali)
- Focus on Kolkata and Jharkhand heritage

---

## 🎨 Core Features & Value Proposition (3-4 minutes)

### 1. **AI-Powered Travel Assistant**
**"Our AI chat assistant, powered by Google Gemini, provides real-time travel guidance. It understands context, provides budget estimates, and offers personalized recommendations based on local knowledge and web research."**

- Context-aware responses using RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation)
- Budget estimation for trips
- Multi-language support
- Voice-enabled with ElevenLabs integration

### 2. **Heritage Walk Audio Guides**
**"Experience Kolkata's heritage sites with immersive audio guides. We've partnered with ElevenLabs to provide AI-generated voice narration in multiple languages, bringing history to life as you walk through iconic locations like Victoria Memorial, Howrah Bridge, and Kumartuli."**

- GPS-guided audio tours
- Multi-language narration
- Offline-capable mobile app
- Rich historical content

### 3. **AI Itinerary Planner**
**"Our intelligent itinerary planner creates personalized travel plans based on your interests, budget, duration, and travel style. It considers weather, local events, and real-time data to craft the perfect journey."**

- Preferences-based planning
- Budget optimization
- Weather integration
- Real-time updates

### 4. **Transport Tracker**
**"Track Kolkata's iconic tram system and other public transport in real-time. This feature helps tourists navigate the city's heritage transportation network efficiently."**

- Real-time tram tracking
- Route planning
- Historical tram routes
- Integration with train APIs

### 5. **Verified Artisan Marketplace**
**"We've built a blockchain-verified marketplace connecting tourists with local artisans. Every purchase is recorded on the Ethereum blockchain, ensuring authenticity and supporting local craftspeople."**

- Blockchain verification (ETHIndia integration)
- Direct artisan connections
- Authentic craft verification
- Secure payments via Dodo Payments

### 6. **Cultural Experiences**

**"Beyond traditional tourism, we offer unique cultural experiences:"**

- **Artisan Chronicles**: Stories and workshops with local craftspeople
- **Recipe Vault**: Traditional Bengali and tribal recipes with cultural context
- **Patachitra Archive**: Digital preservation of traditional scroll paintings
- **Adda Bot**: AI companion for cultural conversations (Bengali tea-time chat)

### 7. **Heritage NFT & Donations**
**"We're pioneering blockchain-based heritage preservation. Users can collect Heritage NFTs of cultural sites and contribute to Pandal donations during Durga Puja, all verified on-chain."**

- Heritage NFTs for cultural sites
- Transparent donation system
- Blockchain verification
- Community preservation

## 🏗️ Technical Architecture (2 minutes)

### **Frontend**
- **React + TypeScript** with Vite
- Modern UI with Tailwind CSS
- Framer Motion animations
- Multi-language i18n support
- Progressive Web App capabilities

### **Backend**
- **Node.js + Express** REST API
- **Prisma ORM** with PostgreSQL
- JWT authentication
- Role-based access control (Tourist, Guide, Admin, Seller)

### **Mobile App**
- **React Native + Expo**
- Offline-first architecture
- Push notifications
- SQLite for local storage
- Sync capabilities

### **AI & Services Integration**
- **Google Gemini** for AI chat and itinerary generation
- **ElevenLabs** for voice synthesis
- **Axicov** for AI agent orchestration
- **n8n** for workflow automation
- **Dodo Payments** for payment processing
- **Ethereum Blockchain** (Sepolia/Holesky testnets) via ETHIndia

## 🎤 Presentation Tips

1. **Start with a story**: "Imagine walking through Victoria Memorial, hearing its history in your language, guided by AI..."
2. **Show, don't just tell**: Use the live demo to illustrate features
3. **Emphasize the problem**: "Tourists struggle to find authentic experiences and support local communities"
4. **Highlight the solution**: "YatriAI bridges this gap with AI and blockchain"
5. **Be passionate**: Show enthusiasm for heritage preservation
6. **Keep it concise**: Aim for 10-12 minutes, leaving time for Q&A
